How the signal logic works

Every refresh pulls each coin's last 7 days of hourly prices and computes, client-side, in your browser:

RSI(14) — momentum, on a 0–100 scale. SMA(20) vs SMA(50) — trend direction, from the hourly series. Volatility ratio — the last 20 hourly returns' spread compared to the last 100, to catch coins going quiet before a move (a "squeeze").

A BUY needs trend-up (SMA20 > SMA50) and RSI between 45–70 (rising but not overbought) and positive 24h change — three things agreeing, not one AI black box. SELL is the mirror image. SQUEEZE flags low relative volatility, a classic pre-breakout setup, direction unknown. Everything else is WATCH — no strong confluence yet.

This is a stricter bar than most single-indicator "AI scanner" tools, which tend to over-signal. Fewer, more agreed-upon signals is the actual edge here — not more alerts.

Data via the public CoinGecko API, refreshed every 60 seconds. This is an educational technical-analysis tool, not financial advice — it has no view on fundamentals, news, or risk you should be taking. Markets can move against any signal.
